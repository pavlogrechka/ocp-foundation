It enforces the atomic `0.4.0 / Accepted` lifecycle unit while retaining every open question and every activation/candidate/cycle non-implication.

AD-054's executable `assignment-document-acceptance` witness applies the same fixed criteria independently to OCP-005, derives Route F from its own identity/lifecycle/registry surface, binds the exact `0.3.0` reviewed body and seven Accepted consumers, preserves every changed predecessor by data-owned path/hash succession, and enforces the atomic `0.4.0 / Accepted` unit while keeping eight questions, three whole-freeze blockers and OCP-023's completeness need unresolved and unactivated.

The result is readiness only; review, Board authority, atomic transition and exact-head gates remain future-act obligations, with no status, Concept or cycle change.

It preserves every changed live projection as a byte-exact predecessor, proves both forward completion and reviewed atomic rollback are defined, and leaves document promotion, Concept canonicalization, all lifecycle values, open questions, activation and the unmet OCP-023 need unchanged.

AD-057's executable `assignment-document-canonicalization` witness re-evaluates every Canonical criterion on the open cycle, binds the exact Route F authority/dependency/consumer surface and completes only `DOCUMENT_PROMOTION`. It enforces OCP-005 `1.0.0 / Canonical` with Assignment still `Accepted`, preserves all withheld questions and the unmet OCP-023 need, derives OCP-006 L2 pass without lifecycle action, and keeps the Concept step pending under byte-exact predecessor and atomic rollback evidence.
