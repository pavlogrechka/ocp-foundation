It enforces the atomic `0.4.0 / Accepted` lifecycle unit while retaining every open question and every activation/candidate/cycle non-implication.

AD-054's executable `assignment-document-acceptance` witness applies the same fixed criteria independently to OCP-005, derives Route F from its own identity/lifecycle/registry surface, binds the exact `0.3.0` reviewed body and seven Accepted consumers, preserves every changed predecessor by data-owned path/hash succession, and enforces the atomic `0.4.0 / Accepted` unit while keeping eight questions, three whole-freeze blockers and OCP-023's completeness need unresolved and unactivated.
