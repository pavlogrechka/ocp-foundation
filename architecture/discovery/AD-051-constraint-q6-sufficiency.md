# Constraint Q6 Freshness Sufficiency Check

## 1. Gate-first and criterion declared before application

This evidence act is not positive-capable under OCP-016 G4: it adds no rule, result, profile or activation. A definitive duration or replacement-currentness mechanism would be positive-capable and separately G4-gated; a fail-safe deferral would not be.

Q6 may close only if evidence governs every dynamic `ConstraintEvaluationRecord` input, selects exactly one of (a) a duration, (b) a direct statement that duration is unnecessary plus its replacement, or (c) an interim boundary, is supported by a direct owner rule or explicitly exhaustive list, is discriminated executably, and does not contradict current owner text. Silence never suffices.

This calibrates without analogy transfer: Q3 had a direct owner boundary and executable discrimination; Q9 lacked both; Q2 also faced explicit current openness. Q6 is measured on its own evidence.

## 2. Complete current-norm inventory

The executable witness derives the exact structural neighborhood: OCP-006, its six direct dependencies and its nine Accepted direct consumers. Every one of the sixteen is classified by one of the mandated basis types.

OCP-011 alone supplies numeric horizons: `600` and `3600` seconds. Its own text makes them inputs of one exact assessment contract and says no other criterion or consumer may inherit them. OCP-018 likewise says its authorization-local effectivity creates no global lifetime. OCP-013/015/017/019/020/021 provide local fail-safe `current/stale` or equivalent boundaries without a general duration. OCP-000 expressly says contract-local freshness activations create no global evidence lifetime. The remaining documents are silent or defer additional rules; silence is recorded but never counted as evidence.

The OCP-006 record-field list contains `evaluated_at` and distinguishes it from evaluation time, but the list is not explicitly exhaustive as a freshness rule and states no horizon, cutoff or currentness authority.

## 3. Explicit obstacle and executable result

OCP-006 §29 says exactly: “the general freshness question remains open because AD-012/OCP-011 activates one exact assessment kind rather than every dynamic Constraint input.” This is the same general subject as Q6, not a narrower exception.

The executable probe changes only `evaluated_at` to synthetic years 1900 and 2099. Both records remain valid and both still yield `satisfied`; an exact-binding mismatch control yields `indeterminate`. The validator therefore reacts to a real neighboring invariant but does not discriminate record age.

## 4. Decision

The predeclared criterion fails. No general magnitude is established; no direct norm makes magnitude unnecessary by naming a replacement mechanism; and the existing fail-safe absence rule presupposes a determination of `current` rather than defining it. Q6 remains open, `EVALUATION_CURRENTNESS_UNRESOLVED` remains the sole whole-document-freeze blocker, and OCP-006 remains byte-identical at `0.3.2 / Draft` with `Concept-Status: Accepted`.

This result neither makes Constraint ready nor selects it, opens a promotion cycle, changes another question, activates a positive model, or authorizes a subsequent act.

## 5. Versioning, migration and rollback

OCP-006 has no contract change and therefore no version change. AD-051 is a new `0.1.0 / Discovery` evidence artifact. No data, reference, schema or consumer migration exists. Rollback removes only the AD-051 document, witness, checker, tests and accounting line.
