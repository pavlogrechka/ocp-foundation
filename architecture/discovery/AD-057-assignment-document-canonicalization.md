# Assignment Document Canonicalization

## 1. Gate-first and exact authority

This act executes only `DOCUMENT_PROMOTION` in the already open `ASSIGNMENT_T6` cycle. It changes governance lifecycle authority without adding or activating an operational rule, result or profile, so it is not positive-capable under OCP-016 G4 and G4 does not apply. Route F is rederived from OCP-005's foundation-wide registered identity, lifecycle and participation authority; it is not inherited from a precedent.

The Architecture Board mandate covers this document step only. `CONCEPT_CANONICALIZATION` remains pending, Concept `Assignment` remains `Accepted`, and `active_cycle_id` remains `ASSIGNMENT_T6`. Exact-head Fable review, Codex adjudication, green CI and a fresh Board authorization must all bind the unchanged proposed head; no earlier gate transfers.

## 2. Current-state criterion assessment

All seven criteria recorded by AD-052 were re-evaluated on the current tree. The Accepted-only `BOARD_ACCEPTS_CURRENT_SEMANTICS` criterion is inapplicable. Route/authority evidence, the complete atomic unit, stable direct dependencies and machine checks are present now. This act itself supplies the separately mandated Board document decision and the proposed binding review lane; lifecycle effect occurs only through the exact-head authorized squash merge.

Every direct OCP dependency, OCP-000 through OCP-004, is already Canonical, so L2 passes without an exception or same-act dependency promotion. Failure of any criterion stops the transition; the executable witness derives each result separately and does not treat AD-055's earlier result as current proof.

## 3. Meaning and exact compatibility boundary

OCP-001 states: **«`Canonical` означає стабільну versioned governance-поверхню. Воно не означає production readiness, authorization, truth, універсальну повноту або незмінність назавжди.»** The transition therefore establishes a stable `1.x` governance/version boundary for the exact §27 surface. It grants no semantic or operational right beyond Accepted; the rights delta is zero.

Q2, Q4, Q5, Q7, Q8, Q9, Q10 and Q11 remain open and withheld. They cannot be inferred from silence or from Canonical status. OCP-023's `assignment_set_complete_for_resource(...)` need remains unmet, its legitimate evaluator remains absent, and `occupied=false` remains unavailable without valid completeness evidence.

## 4. Dependent consequence without action

OCP-006 directly depends on OCP-005. Once OCP-005 is Canonical, OCP-006's mechanically derived Canonical dependency floor changes from fail with blocker OCP-005 to pass with no L2 blocker. Nothing else follows: OCP-006 remains `0.4.0 / Accepted`, is not selected or promoted, and no T7 cycle opens.

All seven current Accepted direct consumers receive lifecycle/version stability only. Their runtime results, inputs, authority and positive capabilities do not change.

## 5. Version, reviewed body and repository state

OCP-001 requires the first Canonical version to be `1.0.0`; therefore `0.4.0 / Accepted → 1.0.0 / Canonical` is the required lifecycle version, not a precedent-based choice. The immutable `reviewed-contract-v0.3.0.md` remains retained acceptance evidence. Governance requires exact-head review of the Canonical transition, but defines no additional Canonical reviewed-body snapshot obligation; this act binds the current primary through its exact-head gates.

The atomic unit includes version, status, readable compatibility surface, exact dependencies, evidence, gate step, live projections, predecessor bindings, snapshot retention, checker/tests and accounting. Byte-exact pre-act copies preserve every changed historical carrier. Rollback is a new reviewed corrective act restoring both `0.4.0 / Accepted` and `DOCUMENT_PROMOTION=pending`, plus every associated projection; partial rollback and history rewrite are forbidden.

## 6. Non-implications

This act does not canonicalize Assignment, complete the cycle, execute or authorize the third step, promote/canonicalize/select another document, close a question, activate a positive model, satisfy the unmet need or authorize a later act. The next cycle step requires its own mandate and four exact-head gates.
