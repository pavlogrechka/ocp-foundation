# Assignment Document Atomic Acceptance

## 1. Gate-first and outcome

This act changes document lifecycle authority without adding or activating a rule, result or profile. It is therefore not positive-capable under OCP-016 G4. It supplies no completeness source, input snapshot, evaluation context or legitimate evaluator and performs no activation. The existing OCP-023 positive need remains unmet.

Each AD-052 criterion applicable to `Draft → Accepted` is satisfied by its own evidence; both Canonical-only criteria are explicitly inapplicable. OCP-005 therefore advances from `0.3.0 / Draft` to `0.4.0 / Accepted`. Assignment Concept status remains `Accepted`.

## 2. Criterion-by-criterion result

1. **Binding review lane — satisfied by the act and its external exact-head gates.** The lifecycle effect exists only after exact-head Fable review, Codex adjudication, green required CI, explicit Architecture Board authorization and authorized squash merge. These external events are marked analytical rather than self-asserted by the repository.
2. **Exact route and authority ledger — satisfied.** The witness names one route, every authority owner, all seven concrete Accepted consumers, all five exact direct dependencies, executable evidence, non-implications and migration/reopening effect.
3. **Board accepts current semantics — satisfied by the mandate and exact-head authorization.** Acceptance concerns the exact reviewed `0.3.0` body plus the readable lifecycle boundary in §§27–28; it does not imply Concept canonicalization.
4. **Atomic document promotion unit — satisfied.** Version, status, compatibility surface, exact dependencies, reviewed snapshot, evidence, live projections and repository accounting form one rollback unit.
5. **Canonical stability checks and separate Board act — not applicable.** The requested target is `Accepted`, not `Canonical`.
6. **Canonical direct-dependency L2 floor — not applicable.** All five direct dependencies happen to be Canonical, but that fact neither replaces nor activates an Accepted-status criterion.
7. **Exact-head authorization and atomic effect — satisfied only by the external gates and authorized squash.** Before merge the proposal has no lifecycle effect; after authorized squash the whole unit takes effect together.

The criterion set is applied exactly as AD-052 records it. Neither open-question count, whole-document-freeze reachability nor an analogy to OCP-006 is treated as an additional criterion.

## 3. Route F, independently derived

Route F follows from OCP-005's live subject: it defines registered Assignment identity, lifecycle, supersession, temporal effectivity and foundation-wide participation derivation, and OCP-000/OCP-002 project the same Concept. Route C is excluded because this is not merely a shared non-Concept contract; Route E because the authority is not a specialization of one other Concept; Route D because the semantics are foundation-wide rather than domain-local; Route I because no external system owns the identity or lifecycle. The OCP-016 precedent guide is recorded as non-proof and performs no automatic reclassification.

The exact authority ledger assigns document semantics to the Architecture Board, registry projection to OCP-000, taxonomy projection to OCP-002, lifecycle rules to OCP-001, routing/activation boundaries to OCP-016, current semantic authority to OCP-005@0.4.0 and reviewed-body authority to `reviewed-contract-v0.3.0.md`.

## 4. Differences from the prior subject

The comparison with OCP-006 was conducted under the separate criterion “current metadata, current open surfaces, current consumers and current positive needs.” Five differences were found:

- OCP-005 has five Canonical direct dependencies; pre-acceptance OCP-006 had six, including then-Draft OCP-005.
- OCP-005 has seven Accepted consumers; OCP-006 had nine.
- OCP-005 has three whole-freeze surfaces, Q2/Q5/Q9; OCP-006 had one, Q6.
- OCP-005 has one current unmet positive consumer need; OCP-006 had none declared.
- OCP-006 is itself an Accepted consumer of OCP-005, while the prior OCP-006 act saw OCP-005 as its Draft dependency.

None changes an AD-052 Accepted criterion: dependency floor is Canonical-only, consumer count is an exact-ledger obligation rather than a readiness threshold, discovery freeze classes are not governance promotion criteria, and the unmet need remains unmet because no activation occurs. The differences are nevertheless retained as analytical evidence and are not collapsed into “same as OCP-006.”

## 5. Meaning of Accepted and seven consumer effects

Dependents may build on the existing Assignment identity/reference kernel, transition-history lifecycle, structural role/provenance kernel, single-interval effectivity and participation derivation, non-inheritance/non-authority boundary, supersession identity boundary and executable validation boundary. Acceptance grants lifecycle assurance for those existing surfaces. It creates no field, rule, result, profile, evaluator, completeness source or runtime behavior.

The seven current Accepted direct consumers are OCP-006, OCP-013, OCP-015, OCP-017, OCP-020, OCP-021 and OCP-023. The executable ledger binds each to its exact consumed token. For each, the only effect is lifecycle assurance: references remain unversioned, schemas and behavior are unchanged, and no previously conditional positive behavior becomes legitimate.

OCP-023's `RESOURCE_OCCUPANCY_ASSIGNMENT_SET_COMPLETENESS` need remains unmet. Acceptance permits OCP-023 to rely on existing Assignment identity and `assignment_effective_at`, but supplies neither a complete set, a legitimate evaluator nor a G4 activation. Consequently `occupied=false` remains unavailable without independent completeness evidence.

## 6. Open surfaces remain outside the grant

Q2, Q4, Q5, Q7, Q8, Q9, Q10 and Q11 remain open. Dependents receive no amendment model after Establishment, Core role taxonomy, composite-Resource partial-scope identity, specialized coordination/approval/observation role type, Assignment-conflict rule, multiple applicability intervals, canonical provenance taxonomy or replacement overlap/gap policy.

Q2 and Q9 have already failed their own sufficiency tests: current rules do not distinguish supersession-only change from post-Establishment prohibition, and the single interval record shape is not a normative cardinality decision. `Accepted` status gives neither proposition new force. The three whole-freeze blockers remain; document acceptance is not whole-document freeze or Canonical readiness.

Historical discovery results and baseline objects remain byte-identical. ## 8. Version, migration and rollback

`0.3.0 → 0.4.0` is a pre-Canonical MINOR. The semantic contract is unchanged, but lifecycle authority changes what dependents may treat as accepted foundation; this is not editorial PATCH work and removes no accepted contract, so it is not MAJOR.

There is no data, reference, schema or runtime migration. Rollback is one unit: restore `0.3.0 / Draft`, remove §§27–28 and the current Accepted snapshot entry, restore all live projections from the exact pre-acceptance predecessors, and remove AD-054, its witness, checker, tests and accounting together. Partial rollback is invalid.

## 9. Non-implications

This act does not make OCP-005 Canonical; promote or canonicalize any other document, including OCP-006; close any question; activate a positive model; change any Concept or Concept status; satisfy the recorded unmet need; select a candidate; start a promotion cycle; authorize a next act; modify OCP-016; or alter AD-052's criteria. Event remains the only completed cycle and `active_cycle_id` remains null. Any subsequent lifecycle step requires its own mandate and fresh exact-head gates.
