# AD-023 — Positive Conflict Activation Gate

## 1. Mandate and substantive result

AB-018 asks whether Conflict between Operations is a fact, a derived projection or a fundamental Concept. The authorized positive-half act must first attempt to open OCP-019 §9 under OCP-016 G4. Positive models may be compared only after one real Accepted consumer exact-binds all required activation elements.

The gate is **closed** on the exact baseline. No current artifact declares a complete positive Conflict use with a versioned rule, exact input and context, legitimate criterion owner/evaluator and object-class decision. This is the result of the act, not a deferral by preference. The act therefore does not compare positive outcomes, select a positive model or prepare a positive OCP-019 extension. It records every current Accepted-or-higher candidate, names each missing element for every plausible consumer and adds executable witnesses that incomplete or self-declared activation remains `indeterminate`.

AD-023 is `0.1.0 / Discovery`. Making it Accepted while AB-018 remains Open would falsely imply a resolved Board outcome. Merge fixes only this exact-baseline gate finding and its shortest lawful reopening paths.

## 3. Gate applied before model comparison

OCP-016 G4 requires each positive-capable activation to exact-bind one Accepted consumer, baseline, rule version, input snapshot, evaluation context and legitimate owner/evaluator. It forbids inheritance across activations, baseline rewrite and authority transfer by matching labels. OCP-019 §9 adds the consumer's explicit positive result need and requires an object-class decision among a projection, independently identified P-001 record and evidence for a separately admitted fundamental Concept.

The resulting precondition has six checked groups:

1. a concrete Accepted consumer of the positive result;
2. its exact baseline and self-declared positive result need;
3. one versioned positive rule;
4. one exact input snapshot and evaluation context;
5. one legitimate criterion owner/evaluator; and
6. an explicit object-class decision.

All six must come from governed artifacts. Caller fields such as `consumer_status: Accepted` or a claimed owner cannot manufacture authority. A Board preference cannot substitute for the operational consumer. Because the precondition fails, outcome-fair comparison of positive models is prohibited rather than merely inconclusive.
